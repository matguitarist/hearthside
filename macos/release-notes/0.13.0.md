**Forever: WoW Forever with bots on your home server.**

- **A new mode, Forever:** play Blizzard's WoW Forever on your own server with bots, using the Forever client Battle.net installed. Hearthside makes a copy of the client that signs in to your server instead of Blizzard's, and leaves Battle.net's app as it is.
- **Set up from the launcher:** Set Up Forever builds the server on your home server, while this Mac reads the game data from your client (in OrbStack or Docker Desktop) and sends it over. Forever has its own ports, so it runs beside Classic or Pandaria.
- **Signs in for you:** create your account in the launcher with an email and a password. Hearthside keeps the password in your Keychain and signs in at each Play. After a Battle.net update, the next Play makes a new copy of the client.
- **Choose how many bots:** Settings › Forever › Bots sets the most in the world and the most around each player. The bots wake up near players and go back to sleep when nobody is around.
- **The dashboard shows Forever too:** who's on, the bots, memory and CPU, and its latest log lines. You can also start, stop or restart it and change its bots, world threads and rates from any browser at home.
