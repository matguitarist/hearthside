**Ambient occlusion and sun-lit mist, in Survivors and in Pandaria.**

- **Survivors:** corners, the feet of trees and the ground under units are now shaded (ambient occlusion), and a ground mist drifts through the zones, lit by the sun through the trees. O and V switch them in game.
- **Pandaria:** the same two effects are added to your 5.4.8 client, in place of its own ambient occlusion. Your client is prepared again at the next Play.
- **Fog levels in Pandaria,** like WoW Forever's: Settings › Pandaria › Fog goes from Off through Low, Fair, Good and High to Ultra. Each level is smoother, reaches further and has finer light shafts.
- **Change them while you play:** Control-Option-O (ambient occlusion) and Control-Option-M (mist) cycle on, off, then the effect alone; Control-Option-F steps the fog level. AzerothPlus has them too, in a new Graphics tab (/ap).
- **Fixed:** a windowed Pandaria client could open far larger than the screen and show only one corner of the game, magnified. It now fits the screen at every Play.
- **Fixed:** on a home server running CrowdSec, the Pandaria and Classic world servers could not start, because CrowdSec takes port 8085. The world port is now picked once and kept.
