**AzerothAdmin stays out of Battle.net's Forever.**

- **Only while you play on your server:** Hearthside now copies AzerothAdmin into Forever's AddOns folder just before its client starts, and takes it out again when the game quits. Battle.net's own client, which reads the same folder, never sees it.
- **Nothing left behind:** should the launcher quit while the game runs, it clears the addon at its next start.
