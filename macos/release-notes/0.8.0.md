**Azeroth Survivors is now Hearthside, and it plays Mists of Pandaria.**

- **A new name:** the app now runs three games, so it has a name of its own. Survivors keeps its name as one of them. Your settings, accounts, game data and the server on this Mac come along on the first start.
- **Pandaria:** a third mode, Mists of Pandaria 5.4.8 with bots, on a server built on your home server beside Classic (one runs at a time), played with your own World of Warcraft 5.4.8 for Mac. The launcher copies the client's files to the server and builds it, creates your account, and at the first Play prepares your client: it signs in to your server, runs on current macOS with graphics fixes and AMD FSR sharpening. Settings › Pandaria, the Server menu, the Logs window and Help › Pandaria cover the rest.
- **AzerothPlus and AzerothAdmin for 5.4.8:** installed in Pandaria under the same setting, with a bot panel that makes bots of any class.
- **AzerothPlus 1.1 for Classic:** quests accepted and handed in for you (hold Shift to do it yourself), grey items sold and gear repaired at vendors, a search box on your bags, vendor prices, health numbers and quest progress in tooltips, and a bot party panel (/ap bots) that finds bots of your level nearby and tells your party to follow, stay or attack.
- **A new launcher look:** each mode has its own key art, and the title, modes, Play and the version stay in the same place whichever you pick.
- **Home server:** the auction house bot's settings (whether it trades, its prices and stock) in Settings › Bots & Rates, and a world map of every bot and player on the dashboard.

Hearthside is a new app: install it from the disk image. The old Azeroth Survivors app can be deleted afterwards.
