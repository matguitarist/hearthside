**AzerothAdmin for Forever, and Forever's map on the dashboard.**

- **AzerothAdmin in Forever:** /aa (or its minimap button) opens the game master window on your Forever server. It levels you up, gives gold, switches cheats on and off, teleports you (Zephras and Forever's other new places too), adds items, and has a Bots tab that adds companions of your level, fills your group and starts a random dungeon. It needs an administrator account (Settings › Forever).
- **Only on your realm:** Battle.net's own client reads the same AddOns folder, so the addon stays hidden and silent on Blizzard's realms.
- **Installed for you:** Hearthside puts it in place at every Play, under the same Settings › General switch as AzerothPlus and AzerothAdmin for Classic and Pandaria.
- **Forever's world map on the dashboard:** the Forever page now shows Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor with a dot for each bot and player, like Classic's.
