**Survivors gets talents and achievements.**

- **Talents:** each character has its class's three trees, five real 1.12 talents each, reworked for the mode: more damage, a shorter cooldown, a wider area or an extra target for one of your spells, a stat for all of them, or a spell to start every run with (Pyroblast, Blade Flurry, Holy Shock…). Each difficulty a character wins in a zone earns a point; deeper rows open with points in their tree. The Talents button sits beside Merchant; a reset costs 50 silver.
- **Achievements:** 25 feats for the whole account, from slaying 100 enemies in a run to winning every zone or a Mythic +20, each worth points and a purse reward. A toast shows each one as it's earned; the Achievements button beside Quit lists them all with your progress. What your past runs already did is counted the first time you start.
- **No more walking through trees and houses:** the hero now stops at trees, walls and buildings and slides along them.
- **Explosive orbs** are easier to deal with: single-target spells go for them first, three hits of anything break one, and heroes who fight up close get them within reach.
- **Fixed:** clicks in the Merchant and Talents windows reached the screen behind them.
