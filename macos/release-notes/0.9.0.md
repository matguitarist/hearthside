**Survivors gets difficulties, Mythic+ style.**

- **Normal, Heroic, then Mythic +2 to +20** in every zone, each opened by winning the one before it there. Pick it with the new Difficulty button under the zone list.
- **Harder runs pay more:** more coin brought home, bosses that drop armor more often and from stronger sets (Heroic one tier up, Mythic two, three from +10).
- **Weekly affixes** from +2, as in Mythic+: Fortified or Tyrannical, then Bolstering, Raging, Sanguine or Bursting from +4, and Volcanic, Explosive, Quaking or Grievous from +7. They change every Tuesday; the difficulty panel lists this week's.
- **Alt-Z** (⌥ Option-Z) hides the interface for a clean screenshot.
- **Settings › Survivors** in the launcher: your characters with their best difficulty, the purse, back up, restore or reset your progress, and the camera lock.
- **New Survivors art** in the launcher.
